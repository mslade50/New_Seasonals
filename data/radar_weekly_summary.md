This is no longer a hopeful HALEU startup; it is a signed sole-source federal supplier with a $900M anchor customer whose downstream buyers (SMR developers) are being separately underwritten by DOE loans. The multiple compression from the November 2025 peak of $464 was justified when the story was "if DOE ever writes the check." That check cleared last Tuesday.

**Who's on the other side:** Forced/mechanical selling — 2025-basket momentum funds trimmed after the -62% air-pocket broke the technical structure and factor-exposure limits triggered. Chart is a downtrend (price < 50d < 200d), RSI 55, and none of that reflects fundamentals; it reflects flow. The informed bear case is real — cascade transition takes years, cash burn continues, SMR customer revenue is 2028+. But the market is pricing that already; what it isn't pricing is the elimination of the "will they ever get the money" tail risk.

**Data:** Price $174, market cap $3.4B, -62.5% from 52w high $464.25, fwd EPS FY27 $3.97 (analyst range $1.64–$7.33 across 5 covering), fwd PE 43.8x. $900M DOE cascade contract signed 7/1 plus $15M HALEU storage extension.
