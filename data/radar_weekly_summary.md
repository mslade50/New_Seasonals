## MP Materials — Long
_Policy-underwritten toll asset priced as a commodity miner into a hard forcing function._

That is the wrong framework. DOD holds a ~15% equity stake with a **10-year $110/kg NdPr price floor**, layered on top of Project Vault's $12B critical-minerals stockpile and the Nov 10 sunset of China's paused rare-earth export restrictions. That price floor structurally converts the equity from "miner P&L" to "toll asset with DOD as the anchor customer" — bond-like downside, option-like upside on any China escalation. Consensus 2026 estimates model a 10-analyst path from -EPS toward $0.23 (+195% YoY) and $1.04 in 2027 (+353% YoY); those forecasts already exist, but the market is refusing to give MP a policy-asset multiple until China moves. Nov 10 is when China moves — or extends the pause and forces a Beijing counter-move, either of which is a step-change catalyst.

**Who's on the other side.** Momentum/trend funds selling a stock in a clean downtrend (price $53.90 vs 50d $62.52 vs 200d $62.01, -46% from 52w high, RSI 38, volume light). July's rotation moved retail toward AI-memory + squeeze memes, and MP wasn't in either bucket. Also, systematic longs got shaken out of the whole critical-minerals basket after the June 22 China list initially "worked" and then didn't follow through. Nobody informed is on the short side; this is passive drift, which is the cleanest setup.

**The data.** Price $53.90 (snapshot 6/28), mcap $9.6B. Forward EPS 2026 $0.23 (+195%), 2027 $1.04 (+353%), 10–13 analyst coverage. Trend downtrend but off the June low (52w low $29.58, 52w high $100.25). Nov 10 China deadline is the binary event most books aren't sized for.

**What would change my mind.** China extends the export-control pause past Nov 10 with no counter-measure attached, or DOD publicly renegotiates the $110/kg floor down. Either kills the option-like upside; either would reprice MP right back to commodity-miner math.
