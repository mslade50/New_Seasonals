# Risk Dials: Simplification, Portfolio Integration, Display — Final Synthesis (2026-07-16)

Sources: system map (dial internals, consumer map, evidence ledger, site inventory, portfolio surfaces, two structure/value probes), six critiqued proposals (two per axis). Every recommendation below survived adversarial critique; amendments from the critiques are already applied. Line references were code-verified during the critique passes.

## 1. Executive summary

Everything the dial system has earned operationally flows through one number: the 10d MA of the 63d composite, compared to 50, throttling the FAMILY4 dip-buyers to 0.25x (PIT t=-1.96, p=.057). Ex-FAMILY4, the book is healthy above 50 (+0.43 avgR, +108.5R); the entire measurable high-fragility drag is FAMILY4's -20.9R, and the surgical band already captures it. The 5d dial failed at every threshold, the 21d dial agrees with the 63d on the decision state ~90% of days, and the multiplicative machinery (regime mult, calm mults, decay zeros, x80, dynamic FOMC denominator) is unvalidated scaffolding.

The plan is therefore subtraction around a frozen core. Freeze the sizing series bit-for-bit; delete dead code, dead horizons from display, and evidence-free UI claims; put the one live number at the top of the risk page, sourced from the PIT parquet rather than the deploy-time recompute; run a pre-registered simple-dial shadow in a separate file toward an eventual PIT-gated swap; and validate (or prune) the exposure_leg rules, the only other live consumer. One live-path bug found during this work goes first: daily_scan falls back to the non-PIT, wrong-basis `rd2_fragility_ts.parquet` when the PIT file is missing. No new sizing rule ships now; the OLV mild-band candidacy gets a rewritten pre-registration and a study, nothing more.

## 2. The dial system as-is (brief)

Score per horizon = (sum of signal edge x decay weight / max weight) x 80 x regime mult x calm mult. Seven signals with hand-tuned thresholds; edges are diff_mean values from `data/signal_horizon_stats.json` (now reproducible via `scripts/build_signal_horizon_stats.py`, but the shipped JSON is full-sample). The displayed dial is the 5d rolling mean of the timeseries; the live sizing input is the 10d MA of the 63d column of `data/rd2_fragility.parquet` (append-only PIT since 2026-07-02, written by `daily_risk_report.py` in GHA; earlier history and all of `rd2_fragility_ts.parquet` are recompute vintages that drifted up to ~7 pts).

Live consumers: `frag_risk_bands` (FAMILY4 + 3x Bear Fade at [[50,999,0.25]]; replayed PIT in strat_backtester 3b3, applied in daily_scan 2b), `dial_filters` entry gates (52wh Breakout <30, St OS Sznl <65, fail-closed), and `exposure_leg.py` (25% NAV VOO/QQQ kill on raw-21d>50 or 10dMA-63d>50, 1.25x boost when both raw dials <5). Display consumers: dashboard page, daily email, site risk tab, portfolio what-if adjuster, ML features.

Probe findings: the three horizons are ~1.2 signals shown three ways (PC1 83%); an equal-weight decayed sum reproduces the 63d composite at ~0.85 pearson and the gate at ~88-90% day agreement, so the decay/persistence structure is load-bearing and the fitted weights are mostly cosmetic; the >=50 cut sits near the 87th percentile of the 63d distribution, exactly where book avgR breaks; episodes are clean (13 episodes, median ~19 td, none <=5 td), so whipsaw is a non-issue, concentration is (2021 and 2024 absorb roughly half of throttled days). The dial is a per-trade avgR diluter, not a crash detector: it missed Volmageddon, read max 4.9 through the 2022 bear, and June 2026's worst drawdown happened entirely below 50. Caveat carried throughout: most probe statistics were computed on recompute-vintage series and uncommitted scripts; treat them as structure, not fill-grade precision, and do not enshrine them in docs until a committed script reproduces them.

## 3. Ranked recommendations

### Axis A: Simplify (minimalist and robustness lenses largely agree; where they differ, the minimalist's freeze-first posture wins on cost-benefit and the robustness lens's shadow dial is adopted with its critique amendments)

**A1. Close the `_ts` fallback hole in daily_scan. (S, live-path, do first.)**
`daily_scan.py:2278` falls back to `rd2_fragility_ts.parquet` when the PIT parquet is missing. That file is non-PIT, on the raw basis where the gate expects 5d-smoothed (daily_risk_report.py:743's own comment forbids interchanging them), git-committed, and refreshed only by ad-hoc local dashboard runs, so the fallback serves a random local vintage into live sizing. The staleness guard checks age only, never basis. Fix: PIT-or-1.0x (fail-open is already the designed degraded mode). Note the visible consequence: dial_filters then fail closed, suspending two strategies' entries until the producer recovers; acceptable and loud. Both critiques independently rated this the highest-severity item. Consumers: daily_scan only. Validation: none needed beyond existing staleness print.

**A2. Declare the sizing series frozen; adopt nothing into the live path without the PIT gate. (S, policy, effective immediately.)**
The live input stays exactly `rd2_fragility.parquet` 63d + 10d MA + threshold 50 until a replacement passes a `scratch/pit_reestimate.py`-style re-validation. Freeze scope is five thresholds, not three: 50 in frag_risk_bands and twice in exposure_leg, plus the dial_filters 30 and 65. This explicitly includes not adopting `scratch/signal_horizon_stats_candidate.json` into the live path (re-scoring de-calibrates all five at once). Honest rationale: the surviving FAMILY4 evidence is on the PIT-reweighted series (t=-1.96), and the 0.94 PIT-vs-current correlation shows the throttle tolerates weight perturbation, so this is a cost-benefit freeze (N=74, nothing to gain, five live calibrations at stake), not a claim that any perturbation breaks it. Hardening: embed the stats-JSON hash in the parquet schema metadata (mirrors ledger provenance), written by daily_risk_report in GHA, the only writer.

**A3. Extract the scoring core into a streamlit-free `fragility_core.py`. (M.)**
The pipeline is duplicated three times: the page (L3429-36), `daily_risk_report.py` (L118-25), and `weekly_market_rundown.py` (L153-59); extraction must cover all three. This kills the streamlit-stub hack and moves the side-effect writes (`rd2_fragility_ts.parquet` at L3432, fire history) out of cached paths; write `_ts` explicitly from a script with vintage metadata, and only after A1 lands (the page write is currently the only thing refreshing daily_scan's fallback file). Parity guard: old import path vs new module on the same session's inputs, plus exact match on today's newly appended row. Do not assert against appended history; those rows are frozen vintages and cannot be reproduced by construction.

**A4. Delete dead code, keep the live fallback. (S.)**
`compute_horizon_fragility` is not dead: it is the operative fallback in the page, the email, and the weekly PDF when `compute_fragility_timeseries` returns empty, and the email run is the one that appends the sizing parquet. Deleting it import-crashes the GHA risk report, which stops the parquet appending, which silently reverts FAMILY4 to full size on stale scores. Keep the function exported as an explicit fallback (or delete only after making the failure path loud and dial-omitting in all three consumers). Do delete: the unconsumed Distribution-Dominance-Elevated edge path and its false docstring (document the JSON field rather than touching the frozen stats file), the `main()` call to `save_signal_fire_history` (keep the function; `tests/backtest_put_hedge.py --generate` calls it), and `compute_persistence_context` (verified computed at L3674 and rendered nowhere; its 79%/67% hit rates are N=12-13, never PIT-tested, and the 79% rides the 5d dial that failed every sizing test).

**A5. Collapse computed-vs-displayed horizons: keep computing all three, display one. (S-M.)**
Keep computing and appending 5d/21d/63d (the append reindex, `ml/ortho_features.py`, and the `fragility.json` payload schema all depend on the columns; `tests/test_fragility_append.py` asserts new columns are dropped, so the schema is effectively frozen). Display only 63d + its 10d MA + gate state, in the dashboard, the email, and the site. Evidence: 5d dead at every threshold; 21d ~90% state-agreement with 63d; PC1 83%. Give 21d no "confirm" semantics anywhere (the strongest-LOYO shadow was a selection-picked 21d/21MA variant whose 22 unique trades did zero incremental damage; monitor only).

**A6. Stand up the simple-dial shadow in a separate file, pre-registered. (M, swap decision ~2027 at earliest.)**
Spec: equal-weight sum of the 7 signals with linear decay over the 63d window; no diff_mean weights, no regime/calm mults, no x80, no dynamic FOMC denominator (~35 fitted or hand-set parameters removed, FOMC-entry jump killed, stats-JSON vintage sensitivity killed). Amendments applied from both critiques: (a) it cannot be a new column in `rd2_fragility.parquet` (the append test drops new columns); write `rd2_fragility_simple.parquet`; (b) pre-register the threshold rule now as percentile-match to the incumbent gate's ON rate, no scanning (the probe's 2.95 threshold was fit in-sample); (c) compute agreement against the actual live basis (5d-smoothed then 10d MA), which the probe mis-specified; (d) parallel-run stats must include boundary-whipsaw counts at all three live thresholds (50/30/65 equivalents), since dial_filters fail closed; (e) the quarterly live-agreement stats carry near-zero power; the real gate is a PIT re-run on history, and the shadow's own history is a recompute. Until that gate clears, the shadow changes nothing.

### Axis B: Portfolio (the two lenses are complementary: risk-overlay owns book-level machinery, strategy-sizing owns bands; no conflict to resolve except sequencing of the contract, resolved below)

**B1. Do not dial-condition the caps; codify the negative result. (S.)**
No frag-scaled pooled caps (500L/250S) and no frag-scaled per-strategy 250 cap. This is the failed book-wide throttle re-skinned (PIT t=-0.23; taper cost -11.4R; ex-FAMILY4 hi bucket is healthy), on the costliest surface (four aligned sites, one out-of-repo, scalar-to-series engine change). Action: a paragraph in CLAUDE.md's Daily Risk Caps section (that is where the next person would propose it) recording the negative result. While in daily_scan, fix the stale comment at L697-699 still describing the removed OVS 0.75x tilt.

**B2. Exposure_leg: kill the boost rule now, replay before touching the kill rules, add a staleness guard. (M.)**
The 25% NAV VOO/QQQ leg is the correct zero-premium expression of the dial's index-timing content and it is already live. But of its three rules, only the 10dMA-63d>50 kill has PIT pedigree. The 1.25x boost at raw<5/<5 mirrors the per-trade boost killed unanimously in the 2026-07-02 research; drop it on existing evidence alone. The raw-21d>50 kill is a protection rule resting on an unverified t-stat (the "raw has no signal, t=+0.72" figure and the headline SPY -0.99%/21d edge exist in no committed file); removing protection requires the replay to run first. Pre-register a `pages/exposure_backtester.py` comparison (rule-3-only vs current stack), with two ops constraints stated up front: the PIT segment starts 2026-07-02, so pre-2026-07 episodes are vintage-contaminated near the hard 50 edge; and `_read_dial_readings` has no age check today, so add a max-age guard (>3 sessions treats readings as unavailable, which already fail-safes to leg-skipped). Confirm this guard's convention matches daily_scan's FRAG_STALE_TD handling since two consumers now act on one series.

**B3. OLV: formal candidacy for a mild band ([[50,999,0.5]]), study before any ship. (M study; config-only ship if it passes.)**
The only non-FAMILY4 name clearing 2 sigma (diff -0.450, cluster-boot t=-2.13, P=.018, N_hi=70, still +23.2R positive above 50, hence 0.5x dilution-trim, not 0.25x). The critiques found the evidence summary flattering, and the pre-registration must confront: 47 of 70 hi trades are in 2021 alone, entirely in the recompute vintage; 2026, the only genuinely PIT-scored year, flips sign (hi +0.59 vs lo -0.39); it is 1 significant result from a 13-row scan; and the dial missed OLV's actual worst failure (June 2026 was sector_loss_gate territory). Protocol, rewritten: (1) redundancy vs sector_loss_gate (>~40% loss overlap kills it); (2) PIT re-estimate is THE gate, and bucketing must use the PIT re-scored series with sensitivity at thresholds 45/50/55; (3) LOYO restated as "negative in the majority of hi-years" (only 5 years have hi trades; the original >=7-of-10 was unmeetable); (4) damage dispersed across >=8 hi months; (5) pre-register the sizing-order composition: OLV's `earnings_size_override` is a flat REPLACE applied after step 2b, so as ordered today a dial-60 earnings-window signal gets 10 bps flat and the band silently vanishes; decide replace-then-band vs band-exempt and add it to `tests/test_frag_risk_bands.py` before the study; (6) parity script per `scratch/parity_check_frag_bands.py`, and budget the site fragility adjuster (single-band assumption in `fragility.json`/portfolio.js) into scope or exclude it explicitly.

**B4. 3x Bear Fade: keep its band, log a greppable re-exam trigger. (S.)**
Its band was inherited by family analogy, was not in the PIT-validated FAMILY4 set, and its own hi bucket is flat (t=-0.05, N_hi=17), so the band has zero direct validation; but removal touches four aligned sites plus site prose for a null result. Trigger on 2 new hi-frag episodes (the N_hi>=40 framing arrives ~2039 at current rates), recorded next to the existing "re-examine FAMILY4 at +20 trades" entry in CLAUDE.md/notes.md, not in a code comment nobody greps.

**B5. Confirm permanent exemptions; reject >1.0x bands book-wide. (S.)**
OVS (mildly better above 50; the removed tilt stays dead), LT Trend ST OS (powered null, N_hi=79), St OS Sznl (never reaches power), 3x Overbot Fade and 52wh Breakout (zero hi trades; filters self-exclude, bands structurally unbindable), Sector BO (N=2). No hi-frag boost anywhere: ATR Extended Gap Up's +1.98 is N=5, the low-frag boost was killed unanimously, and multipliers >1.0x would interact with the per-strategy cap in the pro-rata region only aligned live-vs-engine on 2026-07-10. **B6. The fragility-portfolio contract in CLAUDE.md, landed in two steps. (S.)**

Step 2, only after B2's replay resolves: the "only portfolio-level risk state" clause, which is false today while exposure_leg rules 1-2 consume raw series. ## 4. Rejected / not yet justified (institutional memory)

- **Book-wide fragility throttle/taper**: rest-of-book at >=50 shows no significant degradation (p=.47 clustered); aggregate PIT t=-0.23; taper cost -11.4R. Removed 2026-07-02. Dial-conditioned pooled or per-strategy caps are the same hypothesis on a costlier surface; rejected without new evidence (B1 codifies this).
- **OVS fragility tilt**: shipped on z=-3.0, killed by the PIT gate 2026-07-03 (PIT t=-1.34; the z lived in untestable 2016-17). OVS is mildly better above 50; stays fully exempt. The OLV candidacy (B3) is structurally at risk of the same failure mode (2021 concentration in the recompute vintage) and its pre-registration is written to catch it.
- **Put hedges**: "net positive at all thresholds" superseded by skew (+4 vol pts/10% OTM) + haircuts: always-on -7.18%/yr, gated@50 -$36.7k, gated@55 breakeven and fragile to dropping any of 3 episodes. The 21d>=70 put-spread alpha-playbook plan rests on 4 completed events. No options execution path exists. Dead as a roadmap item; the SPY-timing content is monetized at zero premium by exposure_leg.
- **Tactical VXX proxy**: right shape (+$39k/10y, LOEO-stable) but ~$3.9k/yr, fails materiality 10x, p=.22. Parked, not shipped; keep the note findable.
- **21d/21MA/thr36 shadow**: strongest LOYO of anything tested but 85% redundant with the 63d rule, 22 unique trades of zero incremental damage, selected from a 27-cell scan. Monitor only; no "fast confirm" billing anywhere.
- **Trend sleeve fragility gate**: N=16 concurrent months, sleeve -0.23%/mo; monthly rebalance clock vs median 19-day episodes means the gate mostly fires late; state-json delta machinery is the sleeve's fragile joint. Ship a print/log-only counterfactual line at rebalance (never touching `trend_sleeve_state.json`); revisit after ~10 more episodes.
- **Hi-frag boost bands (>1.0x)**: N=5 anecdote; low-frag boost killed unanimously; untested cap interactions. Rejected.
- **Finer sizing ramp below 50**: decile structure breaks only at deciles 8-9; no support for a graded ramp.
- **Immediate composite swap to the equal-weight dial**: ~10% of gated days re-date under any respecification; the incumbent's PIT validation does not transfer. The shadow (A6) is the only sanctioned path.
- **Adopting the candidate stats JSON into the live path**: re-scores every dial and de-calibrates five live thresholds at once. Display/research use only until a PIT-gated migration.

## 5. Sequencing

1. **A1** (daily_scan `_ts` fallback): the only live-path defect; small, loud, no dependencies. Both simplify critiques ranked it first.
2. **C2/C4/C6/C7/C8** (pure display cuts, risk.js and email): shippable immediately, zero interaction with the PIT cluster.
3. **C1 + C5** (payload PR: PIT-sourced sizing_state, exposure passthrough, hedge deletion): the highest user-visible value; depends on A1's convention (PIT-or-nothing) being settled.
4. **A4 + B1's comment fix** (dead-code deletion with the fallback preserved; stale OVS-tilt comment): low risk once the import surface is documented.
5. **A3** (fragility_core extraction, covering all three copies, after A1 so the `_ts` write's removal orphans nothing).
6. **A2 + B6 step 1** (freeze policy + contract, vintage/staleness/pre-registration clauses): policy, effective as soon as written; the "only statistic" clause waits for step 7.
7. **B2** (exposure_leg: boost rule out now, staleness guard, pre-registered replay of the raw-21d kill). Resolves B6 step 2.
8. **A6** (simple-dial shadow file): start accumulating parallel history now; swap decision is a 2027 question gated on a PIT re-run.
9. **B3** (OLV study under the rewritten pre-registration) and **B4/B5** (triggers and exemption comments): research-track, no live changes until the gate clears.

The ordering principle: fix the one thing that can mis-size an order today, then make the display tell the truth about what sizes orders, then delete, then extract, then study. Nothing in steps 1-6 changes a staged order; steps 7-9 are the only paths to a live change and each carries its own pre-registered gate.
