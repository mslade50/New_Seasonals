## WORTH DISCUSSING

1. **1.5 as written sizes the wrong legs.** The WCDS/LT edge is same-day clusters (leg 2..k of a k-signal day), not legs entered into an already-open position. Prior-day-open adds are -0.08R for WCDS (t -0.4) and +0.16R for LT (t 1.3). A live rule keyed on "previous day's open/working rows" gives 0.8x to every cluster leg and 1.2x to the no-edge cell; the engine, sizing same-day candidates sequentially, does the opposite. Re-key on same-day staged count before shipping.
2. **The tilt's PnL claim is a vol-matching artefact.** Raw shipped multipliers walk forward at +0.3 pts/yr, 7 of 13 years, worst year -14% vs equal; the real gain is Sharpe +0.11 and maxDD -1.8 pts. Four of twelve multipliers (OVS, LT Trend, Monday Dip, St OS Sznl) have no determinate direction under a block bootstrap.
3. **The engine gate cannot fail on PnL and is masked on drawdown.** GRM alone supplies +6.15 of the +4 pts; the in-sample tilt supplies 2.4 pts of maxDD headroom inside which flow's +1.3 pt widening disappears; the replay used the first plan's multipliers, not the shipped ones.
4. **Flow up-size: dip_buy is the weak carrier.** With the dial gate it is t 1.7 overall, t 1.0 since 2016, and -0.33R on the 8 hi-flow trades of 2026. Equal-risk gains for all three families are 1.5-3% with years-better near 50%; "17 of 17 years" is the more-risk replay.
5. **OLV depth ladder is a three-year result** (2021, 2025, 2026 hold 70 of 74 legs at 6+ open and 73% of the raw gain); equal-risk it wins 8 of 20 years. Fine to ship behind the clip, but the 2027 Q1 re-read is load-bearing, not decorative.
6. **S.1 retired the drawdown rule when it bound.** 1.875 fails P(DD>15%) <= 5% at every haircut; two of the three replacement walls have no number yet. That is an appetite decision and should be recorded as one.
