## GAP 6: the guard's cost, measured

Replay of WP1 on the ledger at GRM 1.875 (m = 1.25 on every notional): Req_proj = m x [open book at the morning + 1.10 x the day's new entries], WP1 rates (single/sector 15%, broad 8%, small-cap 10%, 3x long 45% / **short 90%**, sub-$16.67 short-stock minimums). Trim factor per WP1, f = 0 when the open book alone exceeds 85% of NLV. Foregone PnL = the trimmed fraction of each new entry's booked PnL (x1.25). Caveats: new entries are ledger FILLS (unfilled staged limits are invisible, so Req_new and the trim count are lower bounds), marks are entry notional, and a trim's effect on later days' open book is ignored (slightly over-counts trims).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $750k base, 2003+ | 1.2 | 0.64 (15 days) | 1,781 | 0.81% | 50% / 129% (2023-02-03) |
| $750k base, 2016+ | 2.0 | 1.22 | 4,113 | 1.27% | 53% / 129% |
| $632k NLV, 2003+ | 2.5 | 1.40 (33 days) | 4,411 | 2.01% | 59% / 153% |
| $632k NLV, 2016+ | 3.6 | 2.25 | 9,928 | 3.07% | 63% / 153% |

Book PnL at m = 1.25: $219k/yr (2003+), $324k/yr (2016+). The plan's "0.5 trims/yr at 1.875" holds on the $750k base and roughly triples on live NLV.

GRM: at 2.25 the cost is 2.1% (base) / 4.6% (NLV); at 3.0 it is 4.2 trims/yr and 6.4% (base), 6.6/yr and 10.0% (NLV). The plan's "2.6/yr at 3.0" is the 45%-rate world (below).

**What the trims are.** Every trim day is a 3x-ETF short cluster (2020-06-09, 2022-08-16, 2023-02-02/03/06, 2023-12-04/06, 2024-04-19): on 2023-02-06 the open book is 15 lev3x short legs = 98 of the 113 points of base. 3x ETF Overbot Fade owns $41k of the $42k foregone (base) and $66k of $105k (NLV); the sub-$16.67 per-share minimum adds at most 2.7 points on any day. **With the 3x short rate at the PM 45% instead of 90%: trims 0.13/yr (base) / 0.72/yr (NLV), foregone ~$0, max Req_proj 80% / 95%.** The whole guard cost is the WP2 "45 vs 90" question.

**The 15% / 49% claim verifies** (gross > 100% of base at m = 1, 2016+: 14.8% of days carry 48.7% of PnL on ledger MTM; 14.8% / 48.1% on `strategy_daily.json`; 15.6% / 43.9% on 2003+). But the guard does not key on gross: relief-off days (Req_proj > 60% NLV) are 0.5-1.4% of days carrying 4-6% of PnL, trim days 0.2-0.9% carrying 2.7-6.4%, and the trim removes only the fraction above the line. The days that set the wall are 3x-short clusters, not the SPY/QQQ dip-buy clusters that own the gross tail (2013-11-04: gross 552% of base, requirement 75%).

## WORTH DISCUSSING

1. **The guard's cost is the 3x-short rate, nothing else.** At 90% every trim day is a 3x-fade cluster and the cost is 0.8-3.1% of book PnL (2.0% on live NLV); at 45% it is ~0.1-0.7 trims/yr and ~$0. WP2's what-if decides whether the guard is a formality or a tax on the 3x fades; if IBKR is at 90%, the cheaper fix is a 3x-fade-specific cap (or the fades routed elsewhere), not a book-wide trim. Also: 2013-11-04's 552%-of-base gross projects only 75% of requirement, so the "gross > 100% NAV owns 49% of PnL" line overstates what the guard touches (relief-off days carry 4-6% of PnL).
2. **The flow rule survives walk-forward, but the shipped constants are 2025-26 lines and the brief's family membership is not the study's.** dip_buy 6 is stable every year; oversold_hold 7 and short_fade 104 are stricter than any pre-2022 walk-forward line. Ship the walk-forward (annual expanding tercile) rule rather than frozen integers, and pin `flow_state.py` membership to the study lib or re-derive the cells under WP8 membership. 2026 is a dip_buy flip year (hi-flow -0.40 vs lo +1.10) on the family that also carries the dial gate.
3. **Retire WCDS's legacy seasonal multiplier before fitting anything on WCDS.** It is inverted against edge (1.5x on the +0.23 bucket, 0.66x on the +0.71 bucket), costs ~0.08 Sharpe and a third of the sleeve's mean/var, lives only in two hard-coded blocks (strat_backtester step 3, daily_scan ~L2882), and the tilt's 0.75 was fit on the degraded series. The adds/solo gap does not depend on it, but the email's 0.73R is the same-day-cluster cell; all adds are 0.53 (t 1.9).
4. **The OLV override is an unevidenced $112k rule guarding a 9-trade deficit.** No study at ship, the pre-print cell is the best-performing offset zone after -21..-11, and the post-print hole is N 9-10 with a 2015+ avgR of +0.10. The prereg-style re-exam should ask whether the override should exist, not only whether the post-print cell needs its own rule.
5. **OLV's raise is the one the evidence does not support today; LT Trend's is noise.** OLV 2026 is below every prior midterm (t -3.3), driven by the overflow tier and the May-Aug stack, and two of its last three years are under the 0.5 line; the trust-only tilt variant cuts it to 0.79. Raising it to 1.17 while excluding overflow longs from GRM and clipping the composite is coherent only if the 2027-Q1 re-read is treated as a real kill gate; a 1.0 hold until then costs little. LT Trend at 1.04 is within noise either way.
