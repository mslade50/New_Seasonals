## The pinned thread (grading rules, post in week one)

1/
Every trade idea on this account is graded by a machine, and the machine
is built to be pessimistic. The rules, in public, before the first grade:

2/
Ideas post with instrument, side, entry, time stop, and the evidence
count, before or at execution. No idea posts after the fact. If it isn't
gradeable from the post alone, it doesn't ship.

3/
Grading: a bar that touches both stop and target books the STOP. A stop
the market gaps through fills at the open, plus slippage. Stops arm the
day after entry. Entry-day targets are never credited. Unfilled limits
book a zero, not a win.

4/
Nothing gets deleted. Wrong calls stay up and get graded like everything
else. Corrections happen in replies. The scoreboard posts on a fixed
schedule, losers first, including ideas I drafted and chose NOT to post.
The filter gets graded too.

5/
Why bother: this niche runs on unverifiable track records and quietly
deleted calls. I can't prove I'm good. I can prove I'm counted. Judge the
count.
