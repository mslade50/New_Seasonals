# Voice calibration examples

Use these as shape references, not fill-in-the-blank templates. Preserve the
source facts for the current task; never import numbers from an example.

## Research note to human post

Research-note version:

> CPI day as a distribution: 314 prints, 56% close green, median +0.13%,
> about 2x a normal day. The mean is +0.03%, dead on baseline. The gap is the
> left tail, with a worst print of -6.4%.

Human version:

> looked at what CPI days do. 314 prints: 56% green, median +0.13%, about 2x a
> normal day. sounds great until you check the mean, +0.03%, dead on baseline.
> the difference is one ugly left tail, worst print -6.4%. you collect a little
> edge and once in a while it collects you back.

Why it works: the numbers and caveat are unchanged. Curiosity replaces the
report label, and the reaction arrives only after the evidence.

## Compressed doctrine to lived texture

Compressed version:

> Our research pipeline keeps an append-only file of every idea it killed,
> with the reason. 305 lines and growing. Best week: 28 candidates in, 28 dead.
> The file is the product.

Human version:

> we keep a file of every trade idea we've killed, append only, reason attached.
> it's at 305 lines. anything in there is banned from coming back unless
> something structural changed. there was a week where 28 candidates went in
> and all 28 died. i was weirdly proud of that one.

Why it works: it replaces the slogan with a small human reaction. The evidence
does the positioning work.

## Reply-ammo shape

> in case it comes up: PPI lands the day after CPI this week. 55 priors of that
> ordering, 33 closed green, median +0.21%, mean about zero, worst -9.6%. an echo
> of the CPI-day pattern, decent odds with a nasty tail.

Why it works: the opening explains why the number is useful, then gets out of
the way. The final read is proportional to the evidence.

## Anti-patterns

Avoid:

> CPI day isn't an opportunity, it's a left-tail trap. The data tells a clear
> story: elevated median, muted mean, and catastrophic downside.

Problems: contrast pivot, generic authority phrase, balanced triad, lost exact
numbers, and a certainty the evidence does not support.

Avoid:

> Wild stat: CPI days are green 56% of the time! Here's why that matters.

Problems: manufactured excitement, missing N and distribution, and engagement
bait standing in for an observation.
