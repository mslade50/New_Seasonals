Risk allocation & leverage

Linear what-if: scales each strategy's risk allocation (and the whole book via leverage) on the daily PnL stream. Ignores the live book's 250 bps/day cap and other path-dependent sizing effects. R-based stats are per unit of risk and do not move. Leverage alone moves vol / CAGR / drawdown / MAR / dollars but NOT Sharpe, Sortino or PF — risk-adjusted ratios are scale-invariant (mean and vol scale together). Those only move when the per-strategy dials change the mix. The fragility adjuster is a research counterfactual: Step and Ramp apply the selected threshold, floor, and optional boost to all non-OVS trades. It does not reproduce production policy. Production uses per-strategy frag_risk_bands: only Weak Close Decent Sznls, SPY QQQ MonFri Reversion, Monday Dip, and Indices Oversold Bounce scale to 0.25x at a 10d-MA 63d score of 50 or higher; there is no low-fragility boost, and OVS plus the rest of the book stay at 1.0x. What-if curves use per-trade MTM when available (realized-at-exit only as an old-payload fallback); trades before the fragility history begins keep native size.
