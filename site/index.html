Risk allocation & leverage

Linear what-if: scales each strategy's risk allocation (and the whole book via leverage) on the daily PnL stream. Ignores the live book's 250 bps/day cap and other path-dependent sizing effects. R-based stats are per unit of risk and do not move. Leverage alone moves vol / CAGR / drawdown / MAR / dollars but NOT Sharpe, Sortino or PF — risk-adjusted ratios are scale-invariant (mean and vol scale together). Those only move when the per-strategy dials change the mix. Fragility sizing scales each trade by the risk-dial score on its signal date (Step: boost below threshold, floor at/above; Ramp: live-style linear — 63d / MA 10 / thr 25 / floor 0.10 / boost 1.25 reproduces the live schedule). Because it is per-trade, the equity curve drops to the realized-at-exit basis while active. OVS is exempt, matching live; pre-2016 trades have no score and keep native size.
