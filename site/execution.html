Execution Bridge

Live execution console for the link between this site and the local trading agent. It shows agent status, account positions, working and scheduled orders, and provides controls to place stock, futures, and USD-pair FX bracket orders with limit or market entries (plus MOC for stocks), flatten positions, and cancel or modify orders. Actions are previews in dry-run mode and transmit real orders to IBKR when the agent is armed in live mode; always verify the mode banner and selected account first.
