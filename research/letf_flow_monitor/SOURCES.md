# Primary Sources

- [ProShares TQQQ official fund page](https://www.proshares.com/our-etfs/leveraged-and-inverse/tqqq) — confirms the daily 3x objective and links the official NAV history download.
- [ProShares official TQQQ historical NAV CSV](https://accounts.profunds.com/etfdata/ByFund/TQQQ-historical_nav.csv) — example of the endpoint used for all configured funds; contains NAV, prior NAV, shares outstanding, and AUM.
- [Investment Company Institute ETF structure FAQ](https://www.ici.org/faqs/faqs_etfs) — explains that creations and redemptions expand or contract ETF shares outstanding and are primary-market activity.
- [Federal Reserve: Are Leveraged and Inverse ETFs the New Portfolio Insurers?](https://www.federalreserve.gov/pubs/feds/2013/201348/index.html) — documents predictable same-direction daily LETF rebalancing.
- [Federal Reserve: Are Concerns About Leveraged ETFs Overblown?](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econresdata/feds/2014/files/2014106pap.pdf) — derives rebalancing demand including capital flows and shows why flows can mitigate or aggravate mechanical demand.
