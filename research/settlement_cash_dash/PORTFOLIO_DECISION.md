# Portfolio decision: not selected

The frozen SPY settlement-adjusted cash-dash rule passed its narrow statistical
graduation test, but it was **not selected for the strategy book**.

The reason is portfolio redundancy rather than a failed anomaly. From 2003
onward, 58% of the executable pressure-confirmed events overlapped an existing
FAMILY4 position and 57% overlapped an active long broad-index trade. The
unfiltered 2014+ result was also modest (+0.171% per event after 4 bp, t=1.30),
and the T+1 settlement regime contained too little evidence. An LQD extension
failed over the full sample after conservative costs.

This folder is retained as a falsification and research audit trail. The chosen
non-redundant strategy is `research/treasury_month_end/`.
