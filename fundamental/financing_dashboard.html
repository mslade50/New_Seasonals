The filter funnel

What qualifies

At least $5 per share, $10m average and $5m median daily turnover. At least 252 sessions of usable history; above both 50- and 200-session averages. No recent split or listing warning.

20-session return from +15% to +100%; 60-session return from +25% to +200%, at least 10 percentage points ahead of SPY. No more than 25% below the annual high. The upper limits keep extreme spikes out of this view.

Positive six-month operating cash burn, a reported balance no more than 120 days old, and no more than 24 months of reported operating runway. Capex-only cash use does not qualify.

The main list additionally requires a current review of liquid investments, newer releases and financing after the balance date. Unresolved changes stay in Needs review.

These are working research filters, not statistically calibrated trading rules. Runway starts at the reported balance date. It is not estimated cash remaining today.
