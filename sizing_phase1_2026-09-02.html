1.2Overflow-tier longs excluded from the stepship

Today

Proposed

OVERFLOW_RISK_OVERRIDES scaled by 0.8 so effective bps stay where they are today: OLV 20 · LT Trend 24 · St OS Sznl 32 · 52wh 28 nominal (three new carriers, wired like OLV's). 1.4OLV ladder re-keyed to sleeve depthship

Today

0.5 / 0.7 / 1.0 by the ticker's own OLV signal days in the trailing 21 sessions

Proposed

mult = max(recency rung, depth rung). Depth rung by open OLV legs, filled positions plus working T+3 limits: 0 open → 0.5 · 1 to 2 → 0.7 · 3+ → 1.0. Same-ticker adds full size; the 50%-NAV ticker cap stays. New composite clip: the product of tilt × ladder × pullback tilt × flow on any OLV leg is capped at 1.5x pre-GRM.

Why

The current ladder halves 81% of solo legs (0.48R) but also 43 to 49% of legs entered into 3+ open names (0.93 to 1.15R). Replay: +$125k over 21 years raw, +$16k at equal risk, PnL per $ risk 0.790 to 0.820, better worst-21d. The clip is what stopped the composed levers turning June 2026 into the worst episode.

1.9Flow-aware per-strategy daily capship

Today

250 bps effective, always

Proposed

375 bps for every strategy in a family on days the family's trailing-5-session raw candidate count is top-tercile: dip_buy ≥ 6 · oversold_hold ≥ 7 · short_fade ≥ 104. Counted from the same filter masks daily_scan evaluates, before earnings blackouts and T+1 gates, never from staged rows. Breakouts and bear-ETF fades excluded. Reliefs take the max, never the product. Off for the day when the margin guard projects the requirement above 60% of NLV.

Why

The cap binds on 21% of trades and on exactly the good ones (cap-bound OVS 0.62R vs 0.20R unbound; Weak Close 0.96 vs 0.24). Unfitted replay 2010 to 2026: book PnL +3.8%, Sharpe 2.20 to 2.22, maxDD and worst-21d byte-identical. Never ×99: full removal takes short_fade's maxDD from −$44k to −$127k.

1.11Engine confirmation gate for all of Phase 1gate

Rule
One full-ledger replay with 1.1 to 1.10 applied and every cap re-applied (per-strategy, OVS path-2, OLV ticker, cross-strategy clamp), compared to the unmodified ledger over 2010 to 2026 and 2016-07 onward. Pass only if all four hold:
  • annual PnL gain at least half the package replay's: ≥ +4 points of NAV a year at GRM 1.875 versus today;
  • maxDD no worse than today's by more than 1 point at the GRM-1.5-equivalent (the levers, not the multiplier, must not widen the drawdown);
  • worst 21 days no worse by more than 10%;
  • the 2016+ worst episode is not the June 2026 OLV stack.
On fail

Fix the composition (the OLV clip in 1.4, the guard gate in 1.9 and 1.10), not the multipliers, and re-run.

Reference numbers for the whole package at GRM 1.875, 2005 to 2026, from the composed replay: annual PnL 33.0% of NAV (today 24.6%), Sharpe 2.16 (2.00), maxDD 12.5% (12.4%), worst day 7.3% (5.9%), worst 21 days 9.5% (9.5%).
