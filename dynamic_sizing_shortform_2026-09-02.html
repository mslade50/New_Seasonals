What the dial knows

Book-wide edge does not degrade with the dial (PIT t = −0.23, reconfirmed). Book composition does: beta to SPY 0.21 → 0.54, cross-strategy correlation 0.02 → 0.13, effective strategies 4.4 → 2.8. OLV, uncorrelated with the dip-buy family in calm tape, correlates 0.17 to 0.26 with it above 65. The dial is a correlation instrument. The inverse-covariance solution with an index future available says: keep the idiosyncratic book at full size and take the market position to its own Kelly minus the book's embedded beta. At dial 65 to 80 SPY's conditional mean is −29% annualised, so that position is short. Cutting the book shrinks the edge and the exposure by the same factor; hedging removes only the exposure.

Why no throttle

Book variance comes from whether positions are open; positions open when signals fire; signal density is the edge. The best predictor of next month's book vol (trailing signal count, Spearman 0.24) predicts next month's mean just as well (0.22). Scale down ahead of volatility and you scale down ahead of profit. The dial predicts neither (R² 1% and 0.2%).
