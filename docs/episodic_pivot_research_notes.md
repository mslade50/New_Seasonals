# Episodic Pivot research map

Research freeze: 2026-08-24. This note separates Pradeep Bonde's dated variants from the repo's conservative shadow policy. It is a source synthesis, not evidence that any rule has investment edge.

## Bottom line

Bonde's durable claim is causal, not merely technical: a true EP starts with information surprising enough to change the expected trajectory of a company, then produces unusual price and volume. The screen is a nomination device. The difficult step is rejecting ordinary gaps, stale stories, late-cycle news, and events whose upside is already capped.

The public material does not define one immutable setup. It evolved from classic fundamental EPs toward story/DOG events, EP9/EP9M volume discoveries, delayed EPs, liquid institutional variants, and bearish EPs. Entry timing, gap tolerance, stop placement, time stops, and preferred universe all changed. The bot therefore versions these lanes rather than averaging contradictory rules into a score.

## Conflicts deliberately preserved

- Immediate after-hours/premarket entry versus later regular-session/open entry.
- Very large earnings gaps as opportunities versus later evidence favoring gaps below 20–25%.
- Prior-two-day low, EP-day low, and fixed 2.5–4% stops.
- Five-session versus three-session no-progress exits.
- Low-float neglected names versus later liquid institutional EPs.
- Analyst action as low quality versus rare thesis-changing exceptions.
- Catalyst mandatory for a classic EP versus volume-first EP9M/story discovery.

These are separate hypotheses. A future test may compare them, but production code should never silently mix them.

## Evidence hierarchy used by the bot

1. Government/regulator or known issuer-distribution wire.
2. Reputable independent financial news.
3. Two independent secondary sources describing the same catalyst class within a narrow time window.
4. Search result or snippet: discovery only, never evidence.

The bot remains intentionally narrower than Bonde's discretionary practice. Analyst calls, ordinary management changes, rumors, vague products, trial initiation without positive data, capped takeovers, dilution, bankruptcy, and unresolved evidence do not create order previews.

## What the historical result says

The repo's mechanical gap/volume/neglect proxy has weak or negative typical next-open outcomes and a positive 60-session mean driven by a right tail. That does not validate the bot; it rejects the premise that price/volume rules alone are enough. The only credible next test is prospective, timestamped catalyst labeling and shadow execution, with all rejects retained and no tuning on the already-consumed 2024–2026 evaluation slice.
